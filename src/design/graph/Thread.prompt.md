Thread — an edge rendered as a carved groove in the neumorphic surface (dark trench + thin highlight rim, matching Knot's marble language), hanging like rope under gravity (sag proportional to span). Active threads get a faint warm rim glow along the channel; superseded threads render as a shallow dashed groove. Never a straight line, never a glowing line-in-space.

```jsx
<Thread status="active" width={240} />
<Thread status="superseded" width={240} />
```
