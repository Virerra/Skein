Knot — a graph node rendered as a matte marble resting on (active/correction) or sunk flush into (superseded) the neumorphic surface — a soft blurred contact shadow sells the physicality, with only a faint hint of sheen (never glossy/shiny). Never a plain flat dot or hollow ring. Size scales with connectivity.

```jsx
<Knot label="postgres" status="active" size={72} />
<Knot label="mongodb" status="superseded" size={44} />
```
